**AP CALCULUS AB – MODELING VOLUMES OF SOLIDS**
50 points

**Project Overview**

In this project, you will design and analyze a three-dimensional solid using AP Calculus concepts involving:

- Volumes with known cross-sections or volumes of solids of revolution
- Definite integrals
- Geometric modeling

Your task is to help improve Mr. Malchoff's boring lectures and Power Point Slides. Select an example problem from the Chapter 6.3 Lectures that you would like to bring to life. Generate a poster presentation where you compute the volume of the solid using calculus. Also, you must build a visual or physical representation of your solid.

The goal is to demonstrate both mathematical understanding and creativity through modeling.

**Project Requirements**

Option 1: Solid with known cross-sections (General Slicing Technique) – Select a solid whose cross-sections are a geometric shape that has a base built on a region bounded by functions.

Option 2: Solid of Revolution – (Disk Method or Washer Method) Select a solid that is generated by revolving (a) function(s) around an axis to generate a 3D solid.

*Graphs and Functions – 2-dimensional Visuals*

- Clearly labeled graphs and equations of all functions
- Define the base region or bounding functions
- Describe the cross-sectional shape and axis of revolution (where applicable)
- Points of intersection and appropriate intervals

Recommended graphing tools: Desmos & GeoGebra

*Integral Setup and Volume Calculation*

- Justify your choice of method (i.e. general slicing, disk, washer)
- Correctly set up the integral
- Solve and show all necessary work
- Clearly organize algebra and calculus work

*Model Construction*

Create either a physical model or a digital 3D rendering of the solid generated.

Possible materials/tools:

- cardboard
- foam board
- clay
- 3D printing
- CAD software
- digital modeling platforms

Your model should clearly represent the axis of revolution OR the cross-sectional structure.
